# 🚗 IntelligentRepair

IntelligentRepair is an AI-assisted vehicle diagnostics and garage management
platform designed to help drivers better understand vehicle problems and improve
communication between customers and repair shops.

The platform provides an initial AI-based diagnosis through an interactive
conversation with the driver, then helps connect the driver with participating
garages in their area.

## 🎯 The Problem

Many drivers have limited technical knowledge about their vehicles and struggle
to understand what may be wrong before visiting a garage.

At the same time, garages often receive vehicles without enough preliminary
information about the reported issue.

IntelligentRepair helps bridge this gap by collecting relevant information from
the driver, generating an initial AI-assisted diagnosis, and sharing the
information with the garage.

## ✨ Main Features

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Vehicle Diagnosis
- Interactive conversation between the driver and an AI assistant
- The AI asks follow-up questions about symptoms and vehicle behavior
- Generates an initial assessment of the possible vehicle issue
- Helps the driver better understand the problem before visiting a garage

### 🏪 Garage Connection
- Helps users find garages that work with the platform
- Allows the garage to receive preliminary information about the reported issue
- Improves communication between the customer and the garage

### 🚘 Vehicle Dashboard
Users can add and manage their vehicles and access useful vehicle-specific
information, including:

- Recommended tire pressure
- Engine oil information
- Coolant information
- Vehicle maintenance information

### 🔔 Maintenance Reminders
Users can manage automatic reminders for events such as:

- Annual vehicle inspection
- Scheduled maintenance
- Other vehicle-related reminders

### 👤 Personal Area
- Manage registered vehicles
- View previous service history
- View previous diagnostic requests
- Track interactions and vehicle-related information

### 🛠️ Garage Dashboard
Garages have a dedicated dashboard for managing:

- New customer requests
- Open diagnostic cases
- Previous vehicle issues
- Customer and vehicle information
- Request history

## 💡 Project Goal

The goal of IntelligentRepair is to make vehicle problems easier to understand
for everyday drivers while giving garages better preliminary information before
the vehicle arrives.

By improving the flow of information between the customer and the garage, the
platform aims to make the diagnostic and repair process faster, clearer, and
more efficient.
